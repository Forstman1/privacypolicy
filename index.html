Important

Grand Line Deck is an unofficial, fan-made app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the rights holders.

01

Information We Collect

Grand Line Deck is an unofficial, fan-made deck-building and collection utility. We do not require an account, and we do not operate a server that stores your name, email address, decks, or collection data.

02

Local Storage

Decks, collection information, and app preferences created in the app are stored locally on your device using local device storage. We do not receive this locally stored app data. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

03

Advertising and Google AdMob

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to display banner and interstitial advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive information such as:

  • Your IP address, device and advertising identifiers, and approximate location derived from the IP address.
  • Ad impressions and interactions, app interactions, diagnostic information, and performance information.

This information may be used to deliver, personalize or measure advertisements, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve advertising services. Google processes this information under its own privacy policies and terms. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

04

Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before requesting ads. Your available choices may depend on your location and the applicable consent requirements. You can review or change advertising privacy choices when the app presents Google's privacy options.

05

External Links

The app may provide links to official rulebooks or external TCG resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external websites.

06

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain no account or deck data on our own servers. Locally stored app data remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Data processed by Google AdMob is handled according to Google's policies and retention practices.

07

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date at the top.
